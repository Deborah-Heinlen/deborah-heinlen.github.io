A Team Headshot Session

Headshot Day Prep Guide

Look polished and feel confident on November 4

Prepared for the Baird Financial Team

Your headshot is often the first impression a potential client has of you, and a little preparation goes a long way. We will be photographing the Baird Financial team on November 4. This guide covers what to wear, how to prep your hair, and a few grooming tips so you walk in feeling confident and look polished. Read the section for everyone, then the one that fits you.

My goal for the day is simple: I want you to have fun and feel at ease. Feeling nervous in front of a camera is completely normal, and I will guide you through every step, from posing to expression. Come relaxed, and we will get great photos together.

For Everyone

Consistent Team Look

We are using consistent colors and styles across the team. When photos sit side by side on your website, LinkedIn, and marketing materials, matching looks make the team appear polished, unified, and trustworthy, and no one stands out for the wrong reason.

Go Solid, No Patterns

Solid colors photograph best. Patterns can create visual problems in photos and distract from your face.

Check the Fit

The tight crop of a headshot shows small details, so try your outfit on a few days before the session. It should sit comfortably around the neck and shoulders.

The Team Look

For the Men

White shirt, a tie, and a dark jacket

Shirt
A white button-down dress shirt. If you do not wear dress shirts often, try yours on a few days ahead. A well-fitted shirt makes a big difference.
Tie
The tie can be any color.
Jacket
A sportcoat or suit jacket in navy, black, or dark gray. It must be solid, with no patterns.
Underneath
Skip the undershirt. Undershirts can show through a dress shirt or create visible lines.
For the Women

White blouse under a dark layer

Blouse
A white blouse worn under a dark sweater or jacket.
Layer
Keep the sweater or jacket solid, with no patterns. Layers add depth and interest, with the lighter color underneath and the darker color on top.
Neckline
Modest v-necks, boat necks, and crew necks are best. Avoid turtlenecks and tight scarves, which can make your neck look short, and avoid low necklines.
Fit
Avoid baggy clothes that can make you look larger, and avoid clingy or shiny fabrics like thin polyester or velvet.
Underneath
Make sure nothing shows through your blouse.

Hair & Grooming

For the Men

Get your haircut about a week before. Do not cut your hair the day before, because fresh haircuts can look too sharp. A week gives it time to settle and look natural.

Keep your hair clean. Wash it as you normally would, and do not let it go more than a day or two without washing before your session.

Keep facial hair neat. Whether you are clean-shaven or have a beard, make sure everything is trimmed. Pay special attention to nose and ear hair. If you shave, do it carefully to avoid razor burn.

For the Women

Get your haircut about a week before. Do not cut your hair the day before, because fresh haircuts can look too sharp. A week gives it time to settle and look natural.

Keep hair shiny. Use products that add shine and avoid mattifying products like dry shampoo. Style it the way you usually do.

Avoid buns and ponytails. They can make your hair look thin or create uneven shapes. Wear your hair down or in a style that frames your face naturally.

Makeup for the Women

Keep your makeup natural and polished. Go for an everyday look, with attention to these details.

Foundation

Less is more

The camera picks up every detail. Editing can remove blemishes, but it cannot fix a cakey face. Avoid a foundation that covers your skin and creates a cake-like appearance. If you use one, choose a matte foundation that matches your skin tone and blends with your neck and chest. Apply it lightly, and use translucent powder to reduce shine.

Lipstick

Choose a shade just slightly darker than your natural lip color. Avoid bold or very dark lipstick, which can make your lips look thinner, and stay within your natural lip lines.

Eyes

Use neutral eyeshadow colors, with a matte ivory or white shade under the brow to open up your eyes. Skip eyeliner on the lower lash line, since it makes eyes look smaller and older. Use black mascara instead of brown.

Jewelry

Keep it simple and tasteful. Avoid flashy or shiny pieces that reflect light and distract from your face.

Also for Everyone

Wear your hair the way you normally do. Do not try anything new for your headshot.

Glasses. If you wear glasses, make sure the lenses are clean. Avoid lenses with a reflective coating, since the purple tint used for screen glare can ruin your photos.

Quick Checklist

  • Men: white button-down shirt, a tie in any color, and a solid navy, black, or dark gray jacket
  • Women: white blouse under a solid dark sweater or jacket
  • Outfit tried on a few days before the session
  • Haircut done about a week before the session
  • Hair clean and styled the way you normally wear it
  • Men: facial hair trimmed. Women: light, natural makeup with a matte, never cakey, foundation
  • Glasses cleaned, with no reflective coating
  • Rested and ready to feel confident
