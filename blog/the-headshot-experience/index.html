The Headshot Experience: What to Expect Before, During, and After Your Session

Most people don't like having their picture taken.  Here's what actually happens when you book a session, and why most clients leave feeling completely different than when they walked in.

Most people don't like having their picture taken.  If that's you, you're in good company.  Almost every client who walks through my studio door in Hebron, Kentucky says some version of the same thing: "I hate having my picture taken."  By the time they leave, that feeling is gone.

A Quick Look at the Session

You don't need to worry about knowing how to pose or what to do with your hands.  Sessions are relaxed and guided from start to finish, with coaching on expression and posture built into every shot.  If you want the full walkthrough of what a session looks like, from lighting setup to tethered shooting, you can find it here in Behind the Scenes of a Headshot Session.

What Happens After the Shoot

This is the part people ask about most.  Once your session wraps, I select the strongest images and retouch them with a natural, polished finish, nothing overdone.  Delivery is within a week, so you're not left waiting weeks to get images you can actually use.  Depending on your session, you'll leave with one to five finished images, ready for LinkedIn, your website, a resume, or wherever you need to make a strong first impression.

The Real Payoff

For most people, a headshot ends up living on LinkedIn, and that's exactly where it should be doing its job.  A polished, professional photo is often the first impression someone gets of you online, before a resume, before a portfolio, before a single word of conversation.

Sometimes that first impression matters more than you'd expect.  Jacob came in for a session not long ago and later shared that his new headshot played a real part in landing his next job.  It's a reminder that a photo isn't just a photo.  It's how you show up before you've even walked into the room.

Clients often say the experience itself surprises them too.

"Absolutely wonderful experience for my first time ever doing headshots."

Most people arrive a little nervous and leave wondering why they waited so long.

"The entire experience was effortless, professional, and genuinely enjoyable."

Whether it's a new LinkedIn profile, a career move, or simply an updated photo that finally feels like you, the goal is always the same: an image that works as hard for you as you do.

From Nervous to Confident

That shift, from feeling nervous about the camera to actually feeling good about the results, is the whole point.  It's not about becoming a different version of yourself.  It's about a photo that finally looks like the version of you that shows up to do great work every day.  "Wow, that's really me?" is a phrase I hear often, and it never gets old.

Working with Teams, Too

While this piece is about the individual studio experience, I also work with teams and organizations on-site in Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky, bringing the same relaxed approach and professional lighting to your office.  If you're researching headshots for your whole team, my guide to corporate team headshot sessions walks through planning, scheduling, and everything else you'll want to know.

Still Deciding on a Photographer?

If you haven't booked a session yet and want to know what separates a great headshot photographer from an average one, I put together a full checklist in What to Look For in a Professional Headshot Photographer.

If you're ready to trade the nervousness for a photo you're actually proud of, I would love to help.  Book your session and let's create something you're excited to share.
